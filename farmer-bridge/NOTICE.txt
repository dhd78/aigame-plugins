FarmerBridge 0.1.0

Independent local integration for The Farmer Was Replaced.
No game files, game assets, saves, or BepInEx binaries are included.
BepInEx is a separate project: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx
The bridge listens on 127.0.0.1 only and has no authentication; do not expose it to a public network.
